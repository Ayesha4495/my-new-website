Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Kaghaz is a set of free PDF tools that run inside your web browser. This page explains what happens to your files and what information the site does and does not collect.

Your files

When you merge, compress or convert a file, the work is done by code running on your own phone or computer. Your PDFs and pictures are not uploaded to our servers, not stored by us, and not seen by us. When you close the tab, the data is gone from the page.

Accounts and personal data

Kaghaz has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms that collect your name or email address. We do not sell personal data.

Third-party resources the page loads

To work, the page loads some resources from other services. When your browser fetches them, those services can see your IP address and basic browser details, as with any website:

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on the page.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) supplies the open-source libraries pdf-lib, PDF.js and JSZip that process your files in the browser.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and requested page.

These services have their own privacy policies. None of them receive the contents of your files from Kaghaz.

Cookies and advertising

At the moment Kaghaz does not set cookies of its own and does not show ads. If we add advertising in the future, such as Google AdSense, ad partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. In that case we will update this page first, and you will be able to manage ad personalisation in your Google ad settings.

Children

Kaghaz is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on our GitHub page. Please do not attach private documents.
