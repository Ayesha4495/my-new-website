    Pick the right tool for the job

    Short guides on what each tool does well, and where it stops.

    Merge PDF online

    Joining PDFs is the fastest way to send one tidy file instead of five attachments. Add the files, set the order with the arrow buttons, and the pages are copied across exactly as they are. Fonts, links and selectable text survive, because nothing is re-rendered.

    • Useful for invoices, application forms and scanned receipts
    • Rows run first to last, top to bottom
    • Password-protected files must be unlocked first

    Compress PDF for free

    Email limits and upload forms often cap files at 2 MB or 10 MB. This tool redraws each page as an optimised picture, which shrinks scans and photo-heavy files a lot. Choose Smaller file for the lowest size, or Sharper pages when the text must stay crisp.

    • Best for scans, photo albums and slide exports
    • Text-only PDFs may not get smaller
    • Pages become images, so text is no longer selectable

    PDF to Word converter

    Need to edit a PDF someone sent you? The converter reads the text on every page, keeps the reading order, turns larger type into headings, and writes a .docx that opens in Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice. Page breaks are kept so you can compare with the original.

    • Works best on documents made from Word or Google Docs
    • Tables, columns and pictures are not rebuilt
    • Scanned pages have no text layer, so they come out empty

    JPG to PDF converter

    Photograph a form, a certificate or a handwritten page and turn the pictures into one PDF. Each image gets its own page. Choose A4 pages (595 x 842 pt) for printing, or fit the page to each picture to avoid white borders. Phone photos keep the correct rotation.

    • Accepts JPG, PNG and WebP
    • Reorder pictures before you create the file
    • Large photos are scaled to 3000 px on the long side

    Your documents never leave your device

    Most online PDF sites upload every file to a server before they touch it. Kaghaz does the work in your browser tab with open-source libraries, so contracts, ID cards and bank statements stay on your phone or computer. Close the tab and the data is gone.

    Questions people ask

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Every tool runs inside your browser tab. The page loads its code once, and after that your PDFs and photos are only read by your own device.

    Is there a file size limit?

    Kaghaz sets no limit. The practical limit is your device memory, so very large files of several hundred megabytes can be slow or fail on older phones. A laptop handles them better.

    Why is text not selectable after compressing?

    Compression redraws each page as a picture, which is what makes scans and photo-heavy files much smaller. For a PDF that is mostly selectable text, the result can be the same size or larger, and you should keep the original instead.

    Will PDF to Word keep my layout?

    It keeps the text, the reading order, headings and page breaks. Tables, multi-column layouts and images are not recreated. Scanned PDFs contain pictures of text, and this tool cannot read pictures, so those pages come out empty.

    Can I merge a password-protected PDF?

    Not directly. Open the file with its password, save an unlocked copy from your PDF viewer, and add that copy instead.

    Do I need an account or an app?

    You need neither. Open the page in any current browser on a phone, tablet or computer.
